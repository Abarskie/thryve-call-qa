# Product Overview

## Product

AI-powered Call Quality Assurance and Coaching Platform.

## Problem

Businesses with sales agents, appointment setters, customer service agents, and call center teams often require employees to follow a specific call structure.

Managers currently need to manually listen to calls to determine whether agents followed the required process.

This is slow, inconsistent, and difficult to scale.

## Solution

The platform automatically:

1. Receives an agent call recording.
2. Transcribes the call.
3. Identifies speakers.
4. Compares the conversation against the company's call framework.
5. Determines which required steps were completed or missed.
6. Provides evidence from the transcript.
7. Calculates a compliance score.
8. Generates coaching recommendations.

## Primary User

For the MVP:

Cold calling and appointment-setting businesses.

Users may include:

- Business owners
- Sales managers
- QA managers
- Team leaders

## Core Value Proposition

Analyze every sales call automatically instead of manually listening to each recording.
